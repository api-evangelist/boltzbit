# Boltzbit

> Boltzbit builds General Learning Intelligence (GLI) — AI systems that organizations can own, train, and control, learning continuously from proprietary data rather than depending on static, vendor-controlled models. Its product suite forms an "operating system for AI-native software": BoltzHub (a marketplace for AI-powered apps), BoltzAgent (a self-learning assistant for chat, document editing, and code generation), BoltzLearn (an LLM fine-tuning platform for custom models on your own data), and BoltzCloud (multi-cloud deployment across GCP, AWS, and Azure). Boltzbit is backed by Speedinvest.

## Products

- [BoltzHub](https://boltzhub.com/): Marketplace where creators sell AI-powered apps and consumers subscribe to them.
- [BoltzAgent](https://boltzbit.com/products): Self-learning AI assistant — research-backed chat, document editing (Word/Excel/PowerPoint), and code generation.
- [BoltzLearn](https://boltzbit.com/products): LLM fine-tuning platform to build custom models from your own data.
- [BoltzCloud](https://boltzbit.com/products): Multi-cloud deployment backbone (GCP, AWS, Azure) for Boltzbit apps.
